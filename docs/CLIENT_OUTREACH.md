# Finding a client: ideas and messages to send

The course needs a real client who will use the app for at least 3 years (a UNIMAS department, a local business, an agency, faculty staff, a student club or a student entrepreneur). The teaching team verifies the client. Get a meeting this week: the proposal and the pitch link are due **Fri 16 Oct 2026**.

## Ideas that fit (mobile app plus a real AI feature)
| Client (easy to reach) | App | AI feature that adds real value |
|---|---|---|
| Residential college office or faculty facilities | Report broken things (fan, tap, light) with a photo | The AI reads the photo and text, picks the category and urgency, and routes it |
| Small seller (food stall, craft, home business, pasar seller) | Stock and orders in one place | Take a photo of a product and the AI writes the listing text and suggests a price range |
| UNIMAS department or faculty office | Answers the questions students ask all the time | A chatbot that answers only from the department's own documents |
| Student club or society | Event sign-up and QR check-in | The AI sorts and summarises feedback after each event |
| Homestay or small guesthouse | Booking and guest info | A guest assistant in English and Malay |
| Campus recycling or sustainability group | Recycling guide and points | Photo of an item, the AI says which bin it goes in |

Best first three: college maintenance reporting, a small seller's photo-to-listing helper, a department FAQ assistant. They are easy to reach, have visible problems with numbers, use no sensitive data, work with a free-tier AI API, and fit 4 to 5 people in about 10 weeks.

Avoid: medical or banking data, a client who will not sign the approval form, an idea where the AI is only decoration.

## Quick test before you commit to a client
1. Can they meet you this week?
2. Will they sign the course's client approval form?
3. Can they show a problem with a number, an example or a screenshot?
4. Will they still use it in 3 years?
5. Does the AI solve one of those problems, and is not just a chatbot added on?

## Tips
- Message 3 or 4 possible clients today, since some will not reply in time.
- Use the short message for people you know, the email for staff and offices.
- Before the meeting, read `CLIENT_MEETING_GUIDE.md`. Ask for numbers, examples and screenshots: the report is marked on that evidence.
- Mention the client approval form at the meeting. It is part of M1.
- Tell your lab demonstrator your pick early, because the teaching team verifies clients.
