# Client meeting guide (30 to 45 minutes)

Goal: leave with enough real facts to write the proposal. The report is marked on evidence from the client (numbers, quotes, examples), so write down exact words and numbers, and take photos or screenshots if they allow it.

## Roles in the meeting

- **Leader:** opens, keeps time, closes.
- **Planning Manager:** asks most of the questions.
- **Two members:** write notes. One writes quotes word for word, one writes numbers.
- **Everyone else:** may ask a follow-up question. Do not all talk at once.

## Before you go

- [ ] Send a reminder the day before (time, place, how long)
- [ ] Print or open the client approval form from the course
- [ ] Ask permission to take notes, photos and a short audio recording (optional)
- [ ] Bring a phone to take photos of the current process (paper forms, spreadsheets, chat groups)
- [ ] Do not show them a finished design. You are here to listen.

## 1. Open (3 min)

- Thank them. Say who you are, the course, and that the app is free and a student project.
- Say you will ask about how they work now, and that no question has a wrong answer.

## 2. About them (5 min) [fills Report 1.1 and slide 3]

1. What does [ORGANISATION] do, and how long has it been running?
2. What is your role here?
3. How many people work or are involved? How many would use an app?
4. Who would use it: staff, customers, students, members? Roughly how many of each?
5. What phones do they have? Mostly Android? Do they have good internet?

## 3. How they work today (10 min) [fills Table 1 and slide 4]

6. Walk me through a normal day (or one typical case) from start to finish. How is it done now? Paper, WhatsApp, Excel, other?
7. What takes the most time?
8. What goes wrong most often? Can you tell me about the last time it happened?
9. How often does that happen? (per day, week or month)
10. What does it cost you when it goes wrong? (time, money, complaints, lost orders)
11. What have you already tried to fix it? Why did it not work?
12. Can I see an example? (a form, a sheet, a chat, a record) May I take a photo with personal details hidden?

Write down for each problem: the problem, the number, a quote, and who told you.

## 4. What they want (8 min) [fills objectives, user stories, scope]

13. If the app worked perfectly, what would be different for you in 6 months?
14. What is the one thing it must do on day one?
15. What would be nice but not needed?
16. Is there anything you do not want in the app?
17. How will you decide if it is a success? (a number, if possible: fewer mistakes, faster, fewer questions)
18. Are there rules or policies we must follow? (privacy, approvals, branding, language)
19. Which languages should it use? (English, Malay, other)

## 5. The AI idea (5 min) [fills Table 6 and slide 8]

Explain in one sentence the AI idea you have. Then ask:

20. Would this help with the problem you described? How?
21. Who would use it, and how often?
22. What information could we use for it? Is any of it private or sensitive?
23. What should the app do if the AI gives a wrong answer?
24. Are you comfortable with the data going to an outside AI service? What should never be sent?

## 6. Working together (5 min) [fills risks and the approval form]

25. Will you still use this app after the semester? For how long? (the course asks for at least 3 years)
26. Who would look after it afterwards?
27. Can we meet every two weeks for 20 to 30 minutes to show progress?
28. Who is the one contact person we should message?
29. What is the best way to reach you, and when?
30. Are you able to sign a client approval form today or this week?
31. Are you happy for us to use your name and logo in the report and the pitch video?
32. Can we visit again to take photos or see the work in person?

## 7. Close (2 min)

- Repeat back the top 3 problems in your own words and ask: "Did I get that right?"
- Say what happens next and when they will hear from you (a summary within 2 days).
- Thank them.

## Extra questions by client type

**College or facilities office (fault reporting)**
- How do students report faults now? How many reports a week?
- How long from report to repair? What causes delays?
- Who decides what is urgent, and how?
- What details do you need in a report (place, photo, type)?

**Small seller or stall (orders, stock, listings)**
- How do customers order now? How many orders on a normal day and a busy day?
- How do you track stock? How often do you run out or have too much?
- Where do you post products? How long does one listing take?
- Do you sell by photo, WhatsApp, or in person?

**Department or faculty office (questions and forms)**
- What are the 10 questions students ask most? How many per week?
- Where is the right answer written today? Is it up to date?
- Who answers, and how much of their time does it take?
- Which answers can the app give alone, and which must go to a person?

**Student club (events and members)**
- How many events a year, and how many people at each?
- How do you handle sign-up and attendance now?
- How do you collect and read feedback?
- Who runs the club each year? (the app must survive a change of committee)

## Things not to do

- Do not promise features. Say "we will note it and decide with you."
- Do not argue with their answers. Ask for an example instead.
- Do not ask for passwords, personal records or payment details.
- Do not record audio or take photos without permission.
